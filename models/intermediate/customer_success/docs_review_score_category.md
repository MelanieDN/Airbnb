The different review_scores have been regrouped in 5 categories :

    - Premium : score between 4.9 and 5.0 
    - High Quality : score between 4.7 and 4.89
    - Fair : score between 4.5 and 5.69 
    - Below Average : score between 4.0 and 4.49 
    - Low Performing : score between 0 and 3.99 

This categories were difined using the consumer psychology. On Airbnb, a rating below 4.5 is often seen as a red flag.
