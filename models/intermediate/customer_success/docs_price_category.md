The different prices have been regrouped in 5 categories :

    - Budget : price below 80€
    - Mid Range : price between 80€ and 150€
    - Premium : price between 151€ and 300€
    - Luxury : price between 301€ and 600€
    - Ultra Luxury : price over 600€

This categories were difined by combining the data distribution and the consumer psychology.
